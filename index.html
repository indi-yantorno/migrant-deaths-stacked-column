\

Heat drives a summer peak in recorded deaths

\

What are the leading causes of death among recorded migrants in the El Paso Sector, and how do they change across the year, so prevention can reach people at the right time?

\

\

Recorded deaths by month and cause

\ \

Trend insights

\
\
\

3.7×

\

May is the early warning

\

Environmental exposure deaths jump from 7 cases in April to 26 in May, a month before the peak. Outreach that starts in May reaches people before the deadliest months.

\
\
\

70%

\

Heat drives the peak

\

70% of environmental exposure deaths fall in June to August, peaking in July with 98 cases. Summer prevention messaging should lead with heat.

\
\
\

63%

\

Water risks peak in summer too

\

63% of water-related deaths also occur from June to August, with 39 cases in June alone, so water safety belongs in the same summer outreach.

\
\
\ \
\

About the data. El Paso Sector Migrant Deaths dataset (UNM Office of the Medical Investigator and partner sources). Of 709 recorded cases from 2008 to 2024, 706 have a recorded month and are shown. Causes use the dataset's incident-type field, with duplicate labels merged.

\

Limits. Counts combine all years, so they show the typical seasonal pattern and not any single year. Recorded deaths only; remains never found are not counted.
